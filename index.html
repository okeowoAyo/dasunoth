When President Buhari appointed Mr. Sunday Thomas as Commissioner for Insurance/CEO of NAICOM in 2020, it was the culmination of over four decades building Nigeria's & Africa's financial sector from within. He shaped the very regulations, frameworks, and markets that Nigerian businesses navigate today.

After a distinguished tenure — marked by recapitalisation reforms, microinsurance formalisation, and the landmark Insurance Industry Reform Act — he founded Dasunoth Consulting to bring that same regulatory intelligence directly to Nigerian businesses.

Beyond consulting, Mr. Thomas lectures on SME growth, marketing & social media, AI for entrepreneurs, and personal financial management — making him a complete resource for Nigeria's & Africa's business community.
